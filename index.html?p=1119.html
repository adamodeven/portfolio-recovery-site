Hydride

A series of products built to help bikers stay comfortable and hydrated without stopping.

Hydride is a product line that targets bikepackers and endurance mountain bikers who need both storage and hydration throughout their ride. With multiple mounting location options, any biker can use Hydride. It’s trigger-action water dispensing system addresses the complaints about current similar products on that market, stating it is too difficult to draw the water up the straw.
