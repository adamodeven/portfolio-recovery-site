DRNK

A tool for individuals to keep track of and improve their health.

DRNK is a vacuum-insulated water bottle that takes fitness and daily health tracking away from the smartphone (and all its distractions). It’s LED matrix display uses minimal energy for a long lasting runtime, and displays fitness, heart rate, and, of course, hydration tracking.
