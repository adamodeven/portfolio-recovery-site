I’m a dual-degree student in Industrial Design and Mechanical Engineering at Georgia Tech, focused on hardware product development and innovation. My work emphasizes building well tested products by balancing user needs and mechanical feasibility through prototyping and iteration.

My mission is to improve people’s quality of life by creating thoughtful and intuitive products that reduce friction in everyday experiences.

I’m most interested in projects where design decisions are shaped by the physical constraints of mechanisms, materials, electronics, and assembly. Recent work includes assistive medical devices, mechanical testing systems, and consumer products, all developed from early concept to functional models.
