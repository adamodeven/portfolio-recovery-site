Currently working on ➡️

Hydride mechanical design

Planning on designing the frame for injection molding… need to do some research on that.

What I’m thinking for the internals of the system:

  • Linear friction plate (like clutch but sliding instead of spinning) with single spring loaded tooth at the fully open position, to hold open for refill
  • Trigger releases clutch (and tooth) with steel cable
  • Injection molded clear tank
  • Pusher with o-ring spring loaded to compress, clutch prevents compression when trigger released

I’d like to package for a small pcb and battery to integrate a fill level indicator light. Maybe a hall sensor?

So I just realized that I am overcomplicating the mechanism and I can just restrict flow/flow speed at the mouthpiece with the trigger opening a valve rather than controlling pressure. Switching direction on that, will still implement a constant pressure spring and a lock for refilling, but no more steel cable and clutch system.

The initial steel cable idea came from a trigger > progress ratchet type of system to push bursts of water through, but after switching to continuous flow for UX reasons I was still stuck on that steel cable. Just have to make sure the valve at the mouthpiece is robust so it doesn’t leak through under the continuous spring pressure.
