RoboJackets Branding Refresh

A full refresh of the brand and identity guidelines by which the RJK12’s FIRST Robotics Competition team operates.

This project involved the creation of new logos and graphics, updated typography guidelines, documentation of team presentation standards for various audiences, templates for use by team members, and a cohesive, shareable document outlining the proper usage of everything mentioned. The goal was to end off with a new standard of the team appearance that can be implemented and expected without additional training.
