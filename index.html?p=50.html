Step Stool No. III

A modern furniture piece reinterpreting Piet Mondrian’s Composition No. III.

Step Stool No. III is a functional yet visually appealing addition to a room. Designed for private libraries, it features a top step that lifts for use and slides down for elegant storage. Reach the top shelf–in style.
